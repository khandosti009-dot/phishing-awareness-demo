Phishing Awareness Training

Learn to identify and avoid phishing attacks

🎯 What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cybercrime where attackers impersonate legitimate organizations to trick you into revealing sensitive information like passwords, credit card numbers, or personal data.

Goal: This training helps you recognize phishing attempts and protect yourself.

🚩 Common Red Flags

  • Urgent Action Required: "Your account will be closed in 24 hours!" or "Verify now!"
  • Suspicious URLs: Links that don't match the official domain (e.g., "paypa1.com" instead of "paypal.com")
  • Generic Greetings: "Dear Customer" or "Dear User" instead of your real name
  • Requests for Passwords: Legitimate companies never ask for passwords via email
  • Spelling & Grammar Errors: Professional companies proofread their emails
  • Suspicious Attachments: .exe, .zip, or unexpected files
  • Mismatched Sender Email: Official domain doesn't match sender address
  • Too Good to Be True: "You've won!" or "Claim your prize!" offers

📧 Example: Suspicious Email #1

From: support@paypa1.com
Subject: ⚠️ Urgent: Verify Your Account Immediately
Dear User,

Your PayPal account has been compromised. Click the link below to verify your account within 24 hours or it will be closed.

https://paypa1.com/verify-account?token=xyz123

Best Regards,
PayPal Security Team

Red Flags Identified:

🚩 Sender email is "paypa1.com" (with number 1, not letter L) - Domain Spoofing
🚩 Urgent action required within 24 hours - Creates panic
🚩 Generic greeting "Dear User" - Not personalized
🚩 Asks to click link and verify account - Credential theft
🚩 Suspicious URL with "token" parameter - Phishing link

What You Should Do:

✓ Do NOT click the link
✓ Go directly to paypal.com (type in address bar, don't click email link)
✓ Report email as phishing to PayPal support
✓ Delete the email

🔗 URL Analysis

Suspicious URL Example:

https://paypal-security.verify-account.click/?id=user@email.com&redirect=paypal.com

Why it's suspicious:

  • Domain is "verify-account.click" (not paypal.com)
  • Uses a suspicious TLD ".click"
  • Contains user email as parameter (tracking)
  • Tries to redirect to paypal.com to appear legitimate

Legitimate URL Example:

https://www.paypal.com/signin/

Why it's safe:

  • Official paypal.com domain
  • Uses HTTPS (secure connection)
  • No suspicious parameters or redirects
  • Standard path for login

🛡️ Best Practices to Stay Safe

  • Never click links in emails - Type the website address directly in your browser
  • Hover over links to see the actual URL before clicking
  • Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on all important accounts
  • Use a password manager to create unique, strong passwords
  • Keep your software and browser updated
  • Be skeptical of urgency and threats in emails
  • Verify requests by calling the official company phone number
  • Use email filters and spam detection tools
  • Report suspicious emails to your IT department or email provider
  • Educate yourself continuously about new phishing techniques

📚 Additional Resources

  • Check emails at abuse.net to report phishing
  • Verify domain ownership at whois.com
  • Test your password strength at haveibeenpwned.com
  • Report phishing to Anti-Phishing Working Group (APWG)
  • Visit phishing.org for more information
  • Contact your organization's security/IT department for support
